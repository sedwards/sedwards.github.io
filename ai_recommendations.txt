I used relative links so they work well in static site generators like Jekyll / GitHub Pages.

You can later create:

/images/
/case-studies/
/pages/porting-process.md

Your downloads are assumed to live at:

/downloads/

Example:

/downloads/putty-macos.dmg
/downloads/gftp-macos.dmg

site-root
│
├── downloads
│   ├── putty-macos.dmg
│   ├── gftp-macos.dmg
│   ├── macdependency.dmg
│   ├── macdown.dmg
│   └── appbundlegenerator.dmg
│
├── images
│
├── pages
│   ├── downloads.md
│   ├── porting-process.md
│   ├── overview.md
│   └── resources.md
